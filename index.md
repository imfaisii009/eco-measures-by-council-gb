# ECO measures by council: Great Britain local authority data (to end of March 2026)

A clean, machine-readable copy of the official DESNZ statistics on Energy Company Obligation (ECO) home energy upgrades, covering all 350 single-tier and district councils in England, Wales and Scotland.

Interactive table and methodology: [ECO measures by council on ApplyFreeBoiler.uk](https://applyfreeboiler.uk/data/eco-measures-by-council)

Files and the full column dictionary are in the [README](https://github.com/imfaisii/eco-measures-by-council-gb/blob/main/README.md).

Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Source: DESNZ, Household Energy Efficiency Statistics, June 2026.
